Data sources:
Equities — S&P 500 (US, 1928–2025) or MSCI World gross annual returns (Global, 1970–2025), Aswath Damodaran, NYU Stern / MSCI. Switched independently for each side (Classic and SG have their own Market toggle).
Bonds (Classic 4% Rule's bond leg only) — always 10-Year US Treasury Bonds, Aswath Damodaran, NYU Stern (1928–2025), regardless of either Market toggle. No free, reliable UK gilt total-return series was found — see footnote below.
Cash / money-market buffer (Sovereign Glidepath's cash leg only) — US mode: 3-month US T-Bills, Aswath Damodaran, NYU Stern (1928–2025). Global mode: a UK proxy chosen to track money-market fund returns rather than bonds, spliced from the Bank of England's own policy rate 1970–1985 (Bank Rate, then Minimum Lending Rate, then Minimum Band 1 Dealing Rate — successive names for the same rate, date-weighted into an annual average) and the OECD's UK 3-month interbank rate 1986–2025 (which itself transitions through LIBOR to 2021 and SONIA-based rates from 2022), via FRED series IR3TIB01GBA156N.
Inflation — US mode: US CPI, usinflationcalculator.com (1928–2025). Global mode: UK RPI, Office for National Statistics series CZBH (1970–2025).
When UK figures apply: inflation and the cash proxy switch to the UK series above automatically whenever either Market toggle (Classic or SG) is set to Global — even if only one side is switched. Each side's own equity leg (and Classic's bond leg) still follows its own toggle independently.

Footnote — why no UK gilts: the standard free US dataset this tool otherwise relies on (Damodaran's) has no direct UK equivalent. The closest authoritative UK gilt total-return series, the DMS/Credit Suisse Global Investment Returns Yearbook, is a paid product — raw data access runs to roughly £4,000/year — and no free, reliable substitute covering 1928–2025 could be found. Rather than approximate or fabricate a gilt series, Classic's bond leg stays US Treasury-sourced in both modes; this is a known limitation of the Global option, not an oversight.

Note: this tool runs entirely in your browser. Opened from Sovereign Glidepath's Companion Apps section, Section 1 above is pre-filled automatically from your live Pane 1 plan — opened directly (outside the main app), it falls back to the example values shown, which you can edit freely either way.
